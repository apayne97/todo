Capture a job posting → todo app

Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

📎 Capture job → todo
  1. Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (⌘⇧B in Chrome).
  2. Drag the button above onto the bookmarks bar.
  3. On any job posting page, click it. It reads the page’s structured job data (most ATS sites — Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, LinkedIn — embed this for Google Jobs), asks you to confirm the role and company, then adds apply to <company> — <role> to todos.md, tagged with your captureTag (if set), with the posting link, location, salary, and closing date (as due:) attached.
  4. If the page has no structured data, it falls back to asking for the role and company by hand.

Requires the todo app running at http://localhost:4444 — it always is, via the launchd agent.
